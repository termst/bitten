How to play

You are the apple. Everything down here wants a bite. Clear every room, find the lair, drop down a floor.

Doors bite shutA room stays locked while anything in it is still hungry. Kill it all and the doors open. Nothing else works.
Bites showEvery bite takes half an apple and stays on your skin. Out of apples and you are a core.
Length is healthShoot a snake and it gets shorter. A short snake is almost dead.
Break thingsPots and eggs hold apple slices and coins. One per floor hides a gift. Some eggs hatch.
CoinsSnakes drop coins. Spend them in shops, at the tankstation, the kapsalon and the slot machine.
Powerups stayEverything you pick up lasts the whole run. Gold framed ones are legendary.
DowngradesGet bitten in a room and the rot may follow you out. Bosses, springs and the Apple Wash wash it off.
Curses are dealsSomething gets a lot better, something gets worse. Offerings, cursed books and some votes come with one.
Floors and lairsEvery floor ends in a lair. Beat what lives there, then drop into the hole. The last floor ends in daylight.
Read before you takeStand next to anything you can take and a card shows what it does and what it costs. Reading is free.
Achievements payEvery achievement drops a powerup. Many also add new powerups to the pool. Grinding has a point.
It got harderEvery floor has one more room, snakes are a bit tougher, and the new snakes deeper down hit hard.
Move
Left thumb, anywhere on the left half. Or WASD.
Shoot
Right thumb, drag toward a snake. Or the arrow keys, or hold the mouse button.
Dash
With DASH! or as Apploa Vogels. Space, Shift, right click, a double tap on WASD, or a quick double tap on the left half.
Pause
The round button, Esc or P. This guide is on the pause screen too.
Sound
M switches sound on and off.
Auto aim
Code AUTOAIM on the Code tab, or the Auto aim switch in Admin. You only walk, the apple picks a target and shoots. Your own aim always wins.
Buy
Walk onto something in a shop. Walk into a pump or the barber chair. Walk up to the till to sell.
Story
Tap, Space or Enter for the next line. Skip with the button or Esc.

Settings has screen shake, fancy graphics, vibration, pop-up text, aim assist and the powerup cards. Your save code lives there too.

The pillars of a doorway tell you what is behind it.

  • ShopPowerups for 15 coins, legendaries for 35, a whole apple for 6. Cursed goods at half price, the Apple Wash and a reroll machine. Prices are what they are.
  • BP TankstationThree pumps. Each one pushes one number up and another one down, for 20 to 40 coins. The till buys your powerups back, at fair prices.
  • KapsalonOne cut, 8 coins: a small boost for good. One in four is a kappersfail. Nobody will say anything.
  • Klokhuis GymCoach Larspel opens three of four stations: Loopband for walk speed, Bankdrukken for seed damage, Handboog for seed speed, a protein bar for health. The amounts change every visit. One free set per visit, kept for the run. The fourth machine is broken.
  • RechtbankStep onto a plate. Eiser: a good powerup, ten coins or a curse. Gedaagde: walk free, a settlement, or community service.
  • ClubA neon door from the second floor on. DJ Manu-Beau plays inside. Beat him for a legendary.
  • HellclubHard and Nightmare only, from the third floor. Hidden behind a wall that thumps. The devil plays there.
  • TreasureA chest with two powerups. Take one, the other vanishes. From the second floor on, a chest can bite.
  • ChallengeTouch the altar, survive three waves, win a powerup.
  • OfferingA free powerup that costs one apple of health for good, or a curse. Free is relative.
  • PantrySlices and pots, nothing hungry. A break.
  • VoteTwo motions on two lecterns. Walk onto one, the other fails.
  • Slot machineThree coins a pull. Snacks, coins, powerups or the jackpot. Sometimes snakes, sometimes rot. You knew that.
  • SpringStep in once: two apples healed and every downgrade washed off.
  • Cursed booksEach book holds a powerup and a curse. Take one, the others burn.
  • Mini bossOne big gold snake guarding a good powerup.
  • SurvivalTouch the altar and stay alive while they keep coming.
  • VaultA barred door from the second floor on. Its key hides in something breakable on the same floor.
  • PortalA purple doorway on some floors. The Ender Wyrm lives behind it.
  • Secret roomOne wall per floor is cracked. Shoot it open.

Traps and hazards

  • Spikes come up in turns and hurt snakes too. Conveyor belts carry everything along.
  • Cracked tiles cave in during a fight. Shed skins soak up seeds and venom. Roots hold you for a moment.
  • The freezer aisle and black ice are slippery. In a club the lasers sweep the floor: watch the dotted line, then get out of it.
  • Walk or shoot a shopping cart into a snake to ram it. Carts block venom too.

What hunts you

Bosses

  • Harder difficulties give some snakes a trait, and none of them are good. Armored ones shrug off seeds to the face, so shoot their sides. Swift ones are a third faster. Spiteful ones burst into venom. Some regrow when you leave them alone.

Downgrades

Pick one on the Characters screen. Locked ones show what unlocks them.

  • ArcadeFive floors: the cellar, the halls, a supermarket that never closes, the Second Chamber and the garden at the bottom.
  • EndlessThe floors loop forever. Every lap down is tougher.
  • Boss RushEvery boss in a row, DJ Manu-Beau included. Vote for a powerup before each fight.
  • Extreme ChaosThree layers of 10 to 20 rooms. Any room, any snake and any boss can turn up anywhere, even in rooms where they never live. Bosses wait halfway through a layer too.
  • Custom RunBuild your own run: stack floors you have visited, swap in any boss you have met, make a stage boss only, and set rules like apples, damage, fire rate and snake speed. Add up to three powerups you have found as a start gift; picks that clash are blocked. Wins here do not count as escapes.
  • Daily RunThe same dungeon for everyone today, with one twist. Always on Normal.
  • StoriesSTOP DE LOCKDOWN, and after you finish it, ONDER CURATELE. Pick any story you have opened from the Story screen.

Difficulty

  • Easy: an extra apple, softer snakes, fewer downgrades.
  • Normal: the way it was meant to bite.
  • Hard: tougher, faster snakes, two of them with a nasty trait. The Hellclub can show up.
  • Nightmare: one more snake in every fight, three traits, and every bite takes a whole apple. Unlock it by escaping on Hard.
